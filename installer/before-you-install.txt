ShadowDisk 0.2 — read-only disk and partition inspector with change planning

What this version does
  - Shows your physical disks, partitions, volumes and unallocated space.
  - Shows used data, free space inside volumes and unallocated space as a bar, a pie chart or a table.
  - Shows the health status that Windows reports for each disk.
  - Lets you PLAN a shrink, extend or new volume and see a preview of the result.
    Plans are previews only: nothing is applied to any disk.
  - Exports a plain-text report (serial numbers are masked).

What this version does NOT do
  - It does not resize, create, delete, format, initialize or write to any disk or partition.
  - It does not scan for bad sectors. The health status shown comes from Windows and is not a surface scan.

Future versions may add carefully limited changes. No disk tool can guarantee that data is never
lost: hardware faults, power cuts and Windows limitations can still cause problems. Always keep a
backup of anything important.
